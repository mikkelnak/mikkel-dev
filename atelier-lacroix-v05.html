A studio of stillness

We build shelters that listen
to the mountain before they speak.

I — Craft

Detail Holds Weather.

Hand-jointed larch, brushed stone, oiled brass. Each surface is drawn to age slowly, to gather snow without resistance, and to admit only the light the room asks for.

II — Atmosphere

Rooms Tuned
To Altitude.

Ceiling heights, hearths and thresholds are calibrated to the thinner air of 1,800 metres — a quiet acoustic, a longer breath, the certainty of warmth at dusk.

The Studio

A small practice working between two valleys and one lake.

Atelier Lacroix was founded in 2009 by Anne-Sophie Lacroix and Til Brunner. The studio works from two rooms — one above the Inn in Zuoz, one across the courtyard in Zürich — with a deliberate annual capacity of four houses.

Every commission begins with a season spent on site: drawings made by hand, light measured at dawn, the sound of the wind across the larch logged for a full year. Construction follows only when the place has been understood.
