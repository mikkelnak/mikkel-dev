Eleven Variations · MMXXVI
The Collection

Eleven ways
to cut a chalet
from light.

The same brief — a luxury landing page for a fictional Engadin practice — rendered in eleven distinct design dialects. Pick a card. Each opens its own live page.

All variants share the same content beats: hero, "Detail Holds Weather", "Rooms Tuned To Altitude", a selection of three projects, a studio note. Everything else — typography, palette, layout grammar — is what changes.
