Chalets Cut
From Light

Scroll ↓
Practice — Est. 2014
A small studio shaping permanent houses for impermanent light — every chalet drawn to the line of a particular ridge, a particular winter, a particular family.
Material

Detail Holds Weather

Hand-joined larch facades are scribed to the slope and left to silver over a decade. Each lap, each shadow gap is dimensioned for the season it must survive — snow load computed against the angle of the prevailing wind, drip lines hidden inside a one-millimetre rebate. The chalet ages in public; the joinery does not.

Interior

Rooms Tuned
To Altitude

Air at 1,800 metres reads differently against plaster. We work the geometry of each room to the angle of December sun, then build the windows last — frameless, set deep, sized to a single view. Stone underfoot stays cool through August and warm through February; the house, in either season, is simply still.

Atmosphere

Built for the hour the storm passes.

Every project begins on the site, in weather. We move the line until the building stands inside the landscape rather than upon it — until the chalet, seen from the next ridge, is something one might have imagined was already there.

Studio

A practice of twelve, working slowly.

Atelier Lacroix was founded in Zürich in 2014 and now keeps a winter office in Sils-Maria. We accept four private commissions a year and a small number of cultural projects. Every drawing leaves the studio by hand before it is built.

For commissions, write to commissions@atelier-lacroix.ch. For press, write to press@atelier-lacroix.ch. We do not list a telephone number.
